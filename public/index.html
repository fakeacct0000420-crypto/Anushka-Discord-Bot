Modules

🛡️

Moderation

Kick, ban, unban, timeout, untimeout, and purge — all gated behind a dynamic moderator role stored in MongoDB.

⚠️

AutoMod

Warn users and watch the bot automatically escalate through 5 punishment tiers. Full strike history stored per guild.

🚨

AntiNuke

Passively monitors mass bans, kicks, channel & role deletions, and webhook creation. Quarantines offenders instantly.

🎵

Music

Play, skip, queue, loop, and control volume. Powered by yt-dlp with a per-guild queue system and DJ role check.

Strike Escalation Ladder

Strike 1
📩 DM Warning
Official warning sent directly to the user's inbox.
Strike 2
⏱️ 15-Minute Timeout
Native Discord timeout — cool down period.
Strike 3
⏱️ 12-Hour Timeout
Severe final warning before permanent action.
Strike 4
⚔️ Server Kick
User is removed. They may rejoin if allowed.
Strike 5+
⛔ Permanent Ban
No appeal. Logged and banned permanently.
